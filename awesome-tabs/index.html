built for people with way too many tabs open

Why another tab extension?

Most tab extensions do one job. This one does them all.

One for vertical tabs, one for groups, one to save sessions, one to suspend tabs. Four extensions that don't know about each other, and you still lose tabs. Awesome Tabs does all of it in one place, works the moment you install it, and keeps everything on your computer.

Sleeping tabs

Chrome eating up all your memory? Not anymore.

Tabs you haven't touched in 30 minutes go to sleep. They stay right where they are but use no memory and no CPU. Click one and it's back.

  • Music, calls and chat stay awake
  • See at a glance which tabs are awake and which are asleep
  • Put everything else to sleep in one click

Automatic grouping

Where's my tab? In its website's group.

Every GitHub tab lands in github, every Google Doc in docs. Groups are made for you as you browse, and you can add your own rules on top.

  • Press / to search every title and address
  • Groups you make yourself are never touched
  • Close duplicate tabs in one click

Side panel or docked

Chrome's side panel is too wide. Dock it instead.

Chrome won't let its side panel get narrower than about 320px, so your pages get squeezed. Switch to docked mode and the sidebar sits right next to your Chrome window, as slim as you like, and follows it when you move or resize the window.
