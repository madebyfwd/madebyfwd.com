What it does

Your tabs, finally organized

Vertical tabs

Full titles in a sidebar, with pinned tabs, groups, drag and drop, and every window in one list if you want it.

Automatic grouping

Tabs are grouped as you open them, by your own rules or by website. Groups you make yourself are never touched.

Session autosave

Every window, tab and group is saved as you go. Closed a window by mistake, or Chrome crashed? Restore it in one click, or automatically on startup.

Sleeping tabs

Tabs you haven't used for 30 minutes go to sleep and free their memory, then reload when you click them. Calls and music stay awake. Adjustable, or off.

Find anything

Press / to search titles and addresses. Close duplicates in one click, and see which tabs are awake, sleeping or playing audio.

Side panel or docked

Use Chrome's side panel, or a slim docked window that sits against your browser at any width and follows it around.

Your tabs stay on your computer

  • Sessions and settings are stored locally in Chrome. Nothing about your tabs is ever sent anywhere.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • No access to the content of the pages you visit.
  • Read the privacy policy.
