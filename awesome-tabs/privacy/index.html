Awesome Tabs / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Awesome Tabs is a Chrome extension that shows your tabs in a sidebar, groups them, saves your sessions and puts unused tabs to sleep. It is made by FwdWorks (“we”). This policy explains what data it handles.

Your tabs and sessions stay on your computer

To do its job, the extension reads the titles, addresses (URLs) and groups of your open tabs. Saved sessions, settings and related data are stored only in your browser’s local extension storage on your computer.

  • This data is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • We don’t use analytics, tracking or advertising, and we don’t sell data.
  • Website icons are loaded through Chrome’s built-in favicon service, on your computer.
  • You can export your sessions and settings to a file yourself (Settings → Backup). That file stays wherever you save it.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all of this data from your browser.

Email updates (optional)

You can choose to subscribe to occasional emails about Awesome Tabs. Subscribing is optional and the extension works the same without it.

If you subscribe, we receive:

  • your email address,
  • the date and time you subscribed,
  • the wording of the consent you agreed to,
  • the extension version and where you signed up (the welcome page or Settings).

Nothing about your tabs, browsing or sessions is sent.

How it’s stored: in a private Google Sheet owned by FwdWorks, received through Google Apps Script. Google processes this data as our service provider under Google’s privacy policy. We may later move the list to another email service to send the newsletter; it will be used for the same purpose only.

How it’s used: only to send you news about Awesome Tabs. We never sell or share it, and we never use it for anything else.

Unsubscribing: click Unsubscribe in the extension (Settings → Stay in the loop), use the unsubscribe link in any email, or email hello@fwdworks.io. We keep a record that you unsubscribed so we don’t email you again. To have your email deleted entirely, email us and we’ll remove it.

The extension has no access to the content of the pages you visit.

Children

Awesome Tabs isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their email addresses.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above. Significant changes will be noted in the extension’s release notes.
